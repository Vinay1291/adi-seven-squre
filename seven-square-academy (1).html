About the school

Run by educators, built around seven strengths.

Seven Square Academy is managed by the Seven Eleven Education Society and run day-to-day by an experienced principal, teaching staff, and an advisory board of educationists. The school takes its name from an idea: give a child seven strong squares to stand on, and they can build almost anything on top of them.

Classrooms combine CBSE academics with co-curricular enrichment, so students leave prepared for board exams as well as for life beyond them.

  • CBSE curriculum, Nursery to Grade 12
  • Experiential, activity-led learning
  • Digital library & well-equipped labs
  • Safe, well-connected Mira Road campus

Admissions 2027–28

Four steps, start to seat.

Seats are offered across Nursery to Grade 12, subject to availability at each level.

01

Enquiry & registration

Fill the enquiry form or the registration form at the campus office.

02

Entrance test

Students appear for an age-appropriate entrance assessment.

03

Interaction & confirmation

Parents and students attend an interaction session with the school.

04

Documents & fees

Admission is confirmed once formalities and fees are complete.

Registration for 2027–28 is a paid form available at the fee counter; completing it begins the admissions process but does not itself confirm a seat.
